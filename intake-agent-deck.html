The problem

Buying something at work is confusing.

“Where do I start?”
No clear path — people guess, ask around, or give up.
“Is this allowed?”
Policy is buried — easy to buy off-contract by mistake.
“This takes forever.”
Slow and manual — hunting across portals and people.
The cost: stalled requests, off-contract spend, and wasted time.

ProblemCramming every result into the panel buries the good ones.

Appendix · How we kept it honest

Grounding + a test gate before launch.

An unguided LLM drifts. We grounded every answer in ingested policy, then made admins verify in a playground before anything shipped.

Appendix · A path we rejected

Why a wizard, not a config screen.

Exposing every rule at once was powerful but overwhelming — it eroded admin trust. We chose guided steps that build confidence incrementally.
