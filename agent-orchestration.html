Thinking · Multi-agent UX

How I think about multi-agent UX

Work in progress — thinking I’m developing alongside shipped work.

Intake Agent is the shipped piece: one item, one request, a person on the handoff. A multi-item ask, or a use case that doesn’t fit, goes to another spend or procurement agent. That orchestration is still open thinking.

01

Where it breaks

Handoff, conflicting writes, permission

Failure What breaks Design response
Handoff The next agent starts blank. Pass a packet — intent, cited policy, open decision — and show what was inherited.
Conflicting writes Two agents act on the same request. One owner at a time. The other waits.
Permission bleed A step inherits a wider token than it needs. Scope travels with the task. More access is asked, not inherited.

Agent → agent when the packet is complete and the next step is another skill. Agent → human for spend, low confidence, or a tool failure. Requester, manager, and admin do not share one queue.
